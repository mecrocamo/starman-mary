Two things to confirm with design

1
Focus state. The Figma node doesn’t specify a D-pad focus appearance, but every TV platform needs one. The reference proposes a white ring + 1.06× scale over 150 ms. Confirm against the system’s focus tokens.
2
Button normalization. Figma placed Agree and Decline as separate instances with slightly different height, label size, radius, and padding. The component uses one shared button geometry so the pair matches. Confirm that’s intended.
